About JobCheck

A seminar and mini project by Parmeshwar Bankar and Deepak Chavan, guided by Prof. C. V. Bhusare.

How it works

The app combines posting text into a classification pipeline and reports an estimated fake probability with signals to review. When a trained artifact is present, the application loads it once and uses its output. Without one, a phrase-based demonstration fallback is used.

This is a decision aid, not a guarantee. Scam detection can miss sophisticated fraud and occasionally flag legitimate roles.

Verify employers independently before sharing sensitive information.
